Mutation Mitra म्यूटेशन मित्र

A workflow tool for Indian revenue officers who process land record mutation (दाखिल-खारिज). AI suggests. The officer decides. Everything is auditable.

The problem

When land is sold or inherited, the new owner's name must be written into the revenue record (खतौनी). The path today is slow and full of rework.

Files wait

Paper or slow portals. Cases sit between desks and nobody knows which is most urgent.

Late returns

Missing papers or mismatched name, area or गाटा number are found only after days in the queue.

Repeated writing

Officers rewrite the same field notes, summaries and orders again and again.

The four-step flow

Each case moves through four desks. A return always goes back one step, never to the start. A decided case is read-only.

Document check rules AI

Checklist of required papers and a deed-vs-record table. The rules engine flags gata, area, name and owner mismatches instantly. AI reads the deed and extracts fields, each with its source quote.

Field inspection AI

Lekhpal speaks (Hindi) or types a note. It autosaves even offline. AI turns it into a structured report: possession, boundaries, structures, objections, persons met, conclusion.

Review AI human

One screen with flags, the field report and a short Hindi AI summary with evidence-backed risks. Kanungo recommends approve, reject or return.

Decision AI human

AI drafts the order text. Tehsildar edits it, presses a decision button, and confirms a dialog that shows the final text.

Architecture

One Next.js service, one database, one deploy target. The browser never talks to Gemini and never holds the service key.

What happens when someone presses an AI button

AI is an optional helper wrapped in checks. If any check fails, a rule-based fallback answers instead and the officer can still finish the step.

Grounded

Every extracted field must quote the deed. If the quote is not in the text, the field is shown as "unverified".

Logged

Each suggestion is stored, then marked accepted, edited or ignored. The timeline shows it.

Moving a case safely

Every status change goes through one endpoint and one database function, so two people clicking at once cannot corrupt a case.

Plan

planTransition() checks role and stage, validates the saved step data, and requires a reason for returns and rejects, or the order text for approve and reject.

Apply atomically

apply_transition locks the row, refuses a stale stage, updates stage or outcome, writes the history event and closes any open AI suggestion, in one transaction.

Append-only history

application_events has no update or delete permission. It is the audit trail.

Design principles

The officer decides

No code path lets AI change a case's status. Only a human click reaches the transition API.

Works without AI

Every AI feature has a rules or template fallback. With no API key the full flow still works.

Field-first

Mobile layout, 48 px tap targets, Hindi by default, voice optional, drafts survive a bad network.

Auditable

Append-only events, plus a record of every AI suggestion and what the human did with it.

Honest caveats

The synthetic deeds are short and clean, so real scans will score lower. 3 of 20 calls fell back because of free-tier rate limits, not bad output. A plain regex fallback also scores 100% on this easy set, so this shows the pipeline works, not that the LLM beats regex. Tests: 15 unit/contract tests and 2 Playwright flows (Step 1 to Approved across three roles, and a return path).

Assumes Uttar Pradesh process terms. The 45-day SLA and document lists are placeholders to verify with a real officer. All data is synthetic. See the README for run instructions.
